## 3. Identity Kit

### Fonts

Heading font:
Inter

Body font:
Inter

Reason:
Inter is clean, modern, readable, and works well for technical portfolios without distracting from the work.

### Palette

Near-black text:
`#111827`

Near-white background:
`#F9FAFB`

Main color:
`#1D4ED8`

Accent color:
`#14B8A6`

Soft border/background:
`#E5E7EB`

### Logo or Favicon

Simple favicon concept:
A minimal monogram using the initials `CC` or `AC`, depending on final portfolio naming.

Suggested style:
- Rounded square background
- Near-black initials
- Small teal accent dot or line
- No complex illustration

### Style Note

The portfolio should feel calm, sharp, and practical. It should communicate trust, clarity, and technical growth without looking overly corporate or decorative. The design should frame the work: clean typography, generous spacing, restrained color, and real screenshots as the main proof.
